# TeX

The TeX inside formulas (docs/math.html): what the engine's translator (src/tex.js) makes of it, as MathML, and the `tex.*` diagnostics. Luccet's own math syntax, the dollars and `$$` blocks, is in inline-math.txt and math.txt.

## tex/output-inline

spec: math#formulas

A formula is a `span.t-math` holding a `<math>` element, its TeX in `data-tex`.

````luccet
Let $x^2$ be.
````

````html
<p>Let <span class="t-math" data-tex="x^2"><math><msup><mi>x</mi><mn>2</mn></msup></math></span> be.</p>
````

## tex/output-display

spec: math#formulas elements#numbering

Display math is a `<math display="block">` in `div.t-eq`, with its number beside it.

````luccet
$$
E = mc^2
$$ {#eq-e}

By @eq-e.
````

````html
<div id="eq-e" class="t-eq"><span class="t-math" data-display data-tex="E = mc^2"><math display="block"><mi>E</mi><mo>=</mo><mi>m</mi><msup><mi>c</mi><mn>2</mn></msup></math></span><span class="t-eqno">(1)</span></div>
<p>By <a class="t-ref" href="#eq-e">(1)</a>.</p>
````

## tex/safe

spec: elements#safe math#unsupported
options: safe

Under `safe`, formulas translate as usual: no command writes a link or an attribute.

````luccet
$\alpha \href{javascript:x}{y}$
````

````html
<p><span class="t-math t-error" data-tex="\alpha \href{javascript:x}{y}">\alpha \href{javascript:x}{y}</span></p>
````

````diagnostics
1:9-1:14 error tex.unsupported
````
